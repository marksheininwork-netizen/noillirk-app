Contact support

Email marksheininwork@gmail.com. Include the app name, your device, and a description of the problem. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Common questions

How do I start? Tap Home, then Start playing.

Where is my progress? Open Stats. Scores are saved locally on this device.

How do I reset scores? Open More, then Reset scores.

Can I play offline? After the first successful load, the game may work offline on supported browsers.
