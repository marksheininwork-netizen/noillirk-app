Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Data used

Noillirk stores your best score and number of games played locally in your browser/device using localStorage. This demo does not require an account and does not send scores to a server.

Hosting

When you visit the site, its hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own policies.

Contact

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Do not send sensitive information.

Choices

Use the Reset scores control in the app to erase saved game scores. You can also clear browser site data.

Updates

This policy may change if features or data practices change. Contact marksheininwork@gmail.com with questions.
