## Draft product page copy

**Promotional text**  
Flip each word, beat the clock, and solve seven quick reverse-word puzzles.

**Description**  
Can you read a word backwards and spot the original before time runs out? Noillirk is a quick reverse-word puzzle game built for short breaks and personal bests.

Play seven timed rounds. Each word appears in reverse; type the original word before the countdown ends. Your score is saved on your device, so you can come back and try to beat it.

Features:
- Seven word puzzles per game
- A fresh selection from a varied word pool
- A 20-second timer for each round
- Best score and games played saved on your device
- No account required
- Support, privacy, and terms pages included

Noillirk is for entertainment. Scores stay on your device.
